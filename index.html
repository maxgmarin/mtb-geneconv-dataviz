mtb-geneconv-dataviz

This site contains multiple interactive visualizations and ways to interact with the data from the paper (Gene conversion is a key driver of diversity hotspots in M. tuberculosis antigens and virulence-associated loci, https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.26.708061v2). Below is a table of contents of sites/pages with interactive visualizations of the data and results.

Table of Contents

  • NucDivHotspots Viewer

    Browse long-read/assembly alignment support across the 37 nucleotide-diversity hotspot windows, per Mtb151 sample.

  • MtbParalogRegions Viewer

    Browse long-read/assembly alignment support (plus the H37Rv self-to-self homology map) across ~200 paralogous regions in the H37Rv genome, per Mtb151 sample.

  • Mtb151 Gene Conversion Genome Browser

    Explore all detected gene conversion events in an interactive genome browser (built with Gosling.js).

See README.md for how this site is structured and generated.
