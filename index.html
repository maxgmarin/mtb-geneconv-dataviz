mtb-geneconv-dataviz

This site contains multiple interactive visualizations and ways to interact with the data from the paper Gene conversion is a key driver of diversity hotspots in M. tuberculosis antigens and virulence-associated loci.

Below is a table of contents of sites/pages with interactive visualizations of the data and results.

Table of Contents

  • NucDivHotspots Viewer

    Browse long-read/assembly alignment support across the 37 nucleotide-diversity hotspot windows, per Mtb151 sample.

  • MtbParalogRegions Viewer

    Browse long-read/assembly alignment support (plus the H37Rv self-to-self homology map) across ~200 paralogous regions in the H37Rv genome, per Mtb151 sample.

  • Mtb151 Gene Conversion Genome Browser

    Explore all detected gene conversion events in an interactive genome browser (built with Gosling.js).

  • TBP22 PacBio HiFi Resequencing Validation of suspected gene conversion events

    Per-event IGV-reports comparing a target isolate (expected to carry the variant) against a control isolate, across short-read, long-read, and assembly alignments to H37Rv, for 12 candidate gene conversion events in the TBP22/22CI cohort.

  • H37Rv - Repeats, Low complexity sequence, and homology map viewer

    A related, separately-hosted site visualizing repetitive elements, low-complexity sequence, and the genome self-homology map for H37Rv.

See README.md for how this site is structured and generated.
